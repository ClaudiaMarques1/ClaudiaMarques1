# Hello there! 👋

I'm **Claudia**, a Computer Science graduate based in **Cork, Ireland**, with over five years of experience across **industrial automation** and **operations analytics**.

For the past two years I built **SCADA and HMI software** (COPA-DATA Zenon) for biopharmaceutical isolators in a GMP-regulated environment, working alongside PLC and Validation engineers. Before that I spent three years in procurement operations at Johnson Controls, building **Power BI dashboards**, automating reports with **Power Automate**, and developing a **Power Apps** tool from scratch.

This is where I keep my coursework, projects and experiments. Some of it is a few years old and rough around the edges, and I'm happy to say so. I'm always building the next thing.

- Keeping my **SQL** sharp after my database coursework.
